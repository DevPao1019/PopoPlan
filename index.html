How to get the file
  1. On your iPhone, open Health, tap your picture at the top right, then Export All Health Data.
  2. Save the zip to Files (iCloud Drive works), then tap the zip once to unzip it.
  3. Back here, choose Import Health export and pick export.xml inside the apple_health_export folder.

The file is read on this device only. Only daily totals are kept. Large exports take a minute. Re-import any time to update; a browser page can't read the Health app live.

How to use Life Architect

  1. Set up once (2 minutes)Fill in your profile, then open Settings and turn on Daily reminders. On iPhone or iPad, open the share menu and choose Add to Home Screen, and open it from there from now on. Safari keeps a home-screen app's data much more reliably than a normal tab.
  2. Every day, 2 minutesOpen Overview and fill in Today's checklist. Tick what you did, use + and − for water, sleep, protein, deep work and questions, and rate your energy 1–10 before bed. Logging at night is easiest. Missing one day doesn't break your streak; missing two in a row does.
  3. When you planIn Calendar, tap an empty slot to plan a block, or press, hold and drag to set its length. Today's blocks show on Overview. With the MED Cheryl Y2 package on, Study lists every lecture; tick one once you've tested yourself on it, not just read it.
  4. Once a week, about 10 minutesLook at Last 7 days on Overview and pick the habit that slipped most. Import your Apple Health export on Health. If you use it without an account, back up: Settings, Data, Backup .json, and keep that file in iCloud Drive or Google Drive.
  5. Every 4 weeksDo an InBody scan at the same time of day, before eating, and import the CSV on Health. Update your weight in your profile so protein and calories follow.
  6. Where your data livesSigned in, everything is saved to your account and follows you to any device; only you can read it. Without an account it stays in this browser, and clearing website data or switching devices loses it, so back up weekly and use Restore to move it.

Google Calendar sync

Writes the study blocks you plan in the Calendar to your own Google Calendar, so they appear on your phone. Needs a one-time OAuth client ID.

How to get a client ID
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
  2. In APIs & Services, enable the Google Calendar API.
  3. On the OAuth consent screen, choose External, keep it in Testing, and add your own Gmail as a test user.
  4. In Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type Web application. Add this page's address as an authorized JavaScript origin:
  5. Copy the client ID, paste it above, and select Connect. Google will warn that the app is unverified because it is yours; continue.
